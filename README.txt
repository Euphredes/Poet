           Bank 

LII. 
Set your intension on some topic, 
say, "criminal." Now, map each letter 
of the topic to numbers: one through 
nine, using the Generator: 

criminal = 39949513 

Now, keep adding two numbers together 
to ultimately reduce "criminal" to a 
single number: 

3 + 9 = 12 
9 + 4 = 13 
9 + 5 = 14 
1 + 3 = 4 

criminal = 1213144 

1 + 2 = 3 
1 + 3 = 4 
1 + 4 = 5 
4 = 4 

criminal = 3454 

3 + 4 = 7 
5 + 4 = 9 

criminal = 79 

7 + 9 = 16 

criminal = 16 

1 + 6 = 7 

criminal = 7 

Now, map the single number to letters, 
using the Generator: 

7 = G, P, or Y 

Now, come up with a word related to the 
topic, "criminal," that starts with one 
of the available letters: 

G -> gypsy 

At this point, there are two words in 
the bank: "criminal" and "gypsy." Reduce 
"gypsy" to a single number in the same 
aforeshown manner, to then point to 
a set of letters, to then come up with 
another word for the bank. 

After you have created a word bank for 
your liking, write a poem using those 
words that are supported by the 
Square.

Now, I will pull words from a poem, 
St. Gabriel in The Gypsy Ballads, 
by Lorca: 

hot silver. 
don't forget the gypsies gave 
 that suit to you. 
rich in moons and poorly dressed. 
dark wonder of a woman. 
your eyes gleam like the arid 
 landscapes of horse and rider. 
green-almond bullets.

Irish Structure: 
hot silver in her hair. 
this gypsy is here and there: 
rich in moons; 
a dark woman wonders my moods; 
my eyes gleam away from a stare. 

English Structure: 
four green-wood beads. 
I was given this suit. 
brave to lead. 
criminal pursuits. 
not your joker. 
not your mover. 

Spanish Structure: 
a wisp glowing under a swaying headway, 
green-water energy from the albatross. 
he fishes in the river for brook trout, 
poorly dressed, without flying nor 
 trolling. 

Japanese Structure: 
five to one, walking straightaways. 
   all, nothing...
